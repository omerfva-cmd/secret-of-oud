The Beginning Born from a Passion for Scent

Secret of Oud was born from a deep love of fragrance and a belief that luxury should be accessible. Founded in Riyadh, we set out to create a collection of fragrances that honour the rich tradition of Arabian perfumery while speaking to a modern, global audience.

Every bottle in our collection is crafted with care — from the finest ingredients sourced across the world, to the distinctive design that makes each fragrance instantly recognisable.

Our Mission Luxury in Every Note

We believe a great fragrance is more than a scent — it is a feeling, a memory, a statement. Our mission is simple: to create fragrances that make you feel extraordinary, every single day.

From the boldness of Midnight Breeze to the delicate warmth of Tropical Breeze, each fragrance in our collection tells its own story — and we invite you to make it yours.
